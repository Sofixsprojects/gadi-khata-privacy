Foreground service location demo

App: Gadi Khata · Package: com.gadikhata.app · Permission: FOREGROUND_SERVICE_LOCATION

What the video demonstrates

The driver explicitly starts duty tracking. A persistent notification states that the vehicle route is being recorded for the owner. GPS points are stored in local SQLite first, then automatic daily movement and hourly owner snapshots are derived. Records are queued for sync when Firebase credentials and network are available; otherwise the app remains in local-only mode. Tracking stops when the duty ends.

Why the task must start immediately

The route begins at duty start. Delaying or pausing the foreground service would create gaps in distance, stop count, and daily movement summaries. The service is not used for advertising, analytics, background data collection, or tracking after duty ends.
