Account Deletion

App: Gadi Khata · Package: com.gadikhata.app · Developer: Sofixs Private Limited

You can request deletion of your Gadi Khata cloud account and the records associated with it at any time. No fee is charged. See the Privacy Policy for how data is handled.

How to request deletion

  1. Email sofixscompany@gmail.com with the subject Gadi Khata account deletion request.
  2. Include the username (email address or phone number) used to sign in. You may also include your organization name to help us locate the records.
  3. We will verify the request and confirm deletion by email. If we need more information, we will reply within 7 days.

What is deleted

We delete the Firebase Authentication account, the user profile, push tokens, driver/owner membership records, and organization records associated with the requested account. Records queued for sync are removed from cloud storage. Deletion is normally completed within 7 days of verification. Encrypted backups, where applicable, are removed within 30 days.

Local data on your device

Gadi Khata stores records locally in SQLite and works offline. A deletion request removes cloud records; it does not automatically erase local data from a device you still possess. To remove local records, use the app's data options or uninstall the app. A backup file you previously shared must be deleted separately from wherever you saved it.

If you never created a cloud account

No cloud account or server-side record exists to delete. Uninstalling the app removes the local database from that device. You may still email us if you want confirmation.
